# Privacy Policy for Alarmpro

**Effective date: July 22, 2026**  
**Last updated: July 22, 2026**

Alarmpro is an Android alarm clock application developed and maintained by Tariq Sekhri (`Tariq-Sekhri` on GitHub). This Privacy Policy explains how Alarmpro accesses, uses, stores, shares, retains, and deletes information.

## Summary

Alarmpro is designed to work locally on your device. The app:

- does not require an account;
- does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs;
- does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer;
- does not sell or share user data; and
- does not request internet access.

## Information stored on your device

Alarmpro stores the information needed to provide its features in the app's private local storage. This may include:

- alarm times, labels, schedules, groups, enabled states, snooze settings, and selected sounds;
- timer presets, labels, and currently running timer information;
- stopwatch state, laps, and alert marks;
- world clock selections; and
- app preferences such as time format, vibration, notification, speech, and default alarm settings.

Alarm labels and other text are entered by you. Alarmpro uses this information only to provide the alarm, timer, stopwatch, clock, notification, and related settings features on your device.

## Audio files and text-to-speech

If you choose a custom alarm sound, Android's system file picker lets you select an audio file. Alarmpro receives access only to the file you select and stores a local reference to it so the sound can play later. The file is not uploaded to the developer or copied to an Alarmpro server.

If you enable a speech feature, Alarmpro sends the relevant alarm or timer text to the text-to-speech engine selected on your device. A text-to-speech engine is provided by Android or another provider and may process information according to that provider's own privacy policy. Alarmpro does not independently transmit that text to the developer.

## Permissions and device access

Alarmpro may use the following Android permissions and capabilities solely to provide its core features:

- **Exact alarms:** schedules alarms and timers to occur at the requested time.
- **Notifications:** displays alarm, timer, and stopwatch notifications. User-created labels may appear in notifications, including on the lock screen, depending on your Android notification settings.
- **Wake lock:** keeps the device awake while an alarm is ringing.
- **Boot completed:** restores scheduled alarms and active timers after the device restarts.
- **Full-screen intent:** displays the ringing alarm interface when appropriate, including over the lock screen.
- **Vibration:** vibrates the device for enabled alarms and timers.
- **User-selected audio access:** reads only audio files that you explicitly select through Android's system picker.

You can manage applicable permissions through Android's system settings. Denying or revoking a permission may prevent the related feature from working correctly.

## Data collection, sharing, and sale

Alarmpro does not operate a backend service and does not collect, upload, sell, rent, or share your personal information with the developer, advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Android may back up or transfer locally stored app data when device backup or device-transfer features are enabled. Those services are controlled by Android, your device manufacturer, or your backup provider. The developer does not receive or control those backups.

## Data security

Alarmpro stores its data in Android's private app storage, which is protected by Android's application sandbox. Access to user-selected audio is granted through Android's system file picker. No method of local storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, so you should protect your device with appropriate security settings.

## Data retention and deletion

Local data is retained for as long as it is needed to provide the app's features. You can delete individual alarms, timers, groups, laps, and other saved items from within Alarmpro where those controls are available.

You can delete all locally stored Alarmpro data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Copies managed by an Android backup provider may remain according to that provider's retention and deletion rules. Alarmpro has no user accounts or server-side user data, so there is no separate account-deletion process.

## Children's privacy

Alarmpro is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer, the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Alarmpro's features or data practices change. Updates will be published at this location and identified by a revised "Last updated" date.
